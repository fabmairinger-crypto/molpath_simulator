# MolPath Simulator v2.5.0b — Methods Focus patch 01

- Keeps MTB / LAB / RES as the primary simulator modes.
- Adds an independent cross-domain **Methods focus** filter.
- Uses the strict 91-case curation: 44 method-driven cases (MTB 14 / LAB 16 / RES 14).
- Direct focus classes: molecular diagnostics; preanalytics/analytics/QC; research & experimental design; bioinformatics/statistics; digital pathology/AI.
- Method occurrence alone does not qualify a case. Biological/clinical/evidence/ethical/admin-only challenges remain outside the filter.
- Removes the legacy binary `MET` badge from the case metadata line and replaces it with curated focus badges.
- Existing search, area, difficulty, Signature filter, case logic, scoring, assets and course logic are not changed.
- New UI strings are localised for DE/EN/RO/EL/ES/FR/RU/TR/AR/FA/UK inside the isolated patch.
